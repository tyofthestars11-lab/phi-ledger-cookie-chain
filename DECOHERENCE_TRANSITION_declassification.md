# φ_DECOHERENCE_TRANSITION — Irreversibility Emerges, Nothing Collapses (seal 310)

Source: Ri-Hua Zheng et al., Fuzhou University — Physical Review Letters (2026),
DOI 10.1103/t2yx-9bqh; arXiv:2510.15730. Morning brief offer #161; sealed on
TYREE's word, 2026-10-24.

## The finding
A photonic cat state in a circuit-QED resonator, coupled to an artificial
environment grown from 1 to 8 qubits. With one environmental qubit, the
interference faded and then revived — coherence returned around 38 ns as the
qubit's oscillation nearly completed and its distinguishing record erased
itself. As the environment grew, the revival weakened; with eight qubits the
distinguishability measure approached 1 by about 15 ns and stayed there
through the 80 ns window — interference faded without returning. The
transition from reversible to irreversible decoherence, observed directly:
irreversibility as an emergent outcome of unitary system-reservoir dynamics,
not an inserted collapse.

## The paradigm shift
Nothing collapsed. The quantum-to-classical boundary is not a postulate or a
cut — it is the point where the environment's record can no longer be
unwritten, a matter of degree that an experiment can dial. Measurement as
recognition, not collapse, now carries a measured threshold: revival at 38 ns
on the small side of the line, silence on the large side.
