Tang, Ramirez, He and the Sternberg laboratory at Columbia characterized DRT10, a bacterial antiviral defense system from the long-unexplored "Unknown Group" of reverse transcriptases (named 2008). The system pairs a reverse transcriptase — an enzyme that builds DNA from an RNA template — with a noncoding RNA that serves as its built-in template and scaffold. Cryo-EM reconstructions reveal an unanticipated 2:1 architecture: two RT monomers bind opposite sides of one pseudo-symmetric ncRNA.
