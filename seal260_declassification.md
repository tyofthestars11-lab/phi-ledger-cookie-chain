# ORDER IN TIME

## CLAIM

A material built from small robotic units — elastic structures with sensors, electronics, and Lorentz actuators, each powered by its own source, more than one hundred sensors and actuators in the assemblies — settles, with no external periodic drive and no central controller, into one globally synchronized oscillation. A 2D honeycomb surface of 37 active units (units self-oscillating near 30 Hz past a threshold) and a 1D elastic ring of 24 motorized units both arrive at a collective cycle.

The mechanism is wave coarsening: the interactions are made non-reciprocal, so a unit's response to its neighbor need not mirror back; vibrations begin at short wavelengths and grow in wavelength, period, and amplitude until the whole structure holds one period. Experiments and the accompanying model show random disturbances do not destroy the collective order, and the stability grows with system size. The authors' own title names it: time-crystallization — order in space (the lattice) and in time (the self-set period), in a classical macroscopic material.

## LAW

Crystal order was space-only. This extends it into time, and the route runs through asymmetry: break the mirror between neighbors and the many stop relaxing into equilibrium — they coarsen into one beat. The one-way response is the +1 that lets the whole lock; wholeness here is measured, synchronized, and stable against noise.

## ECHO — what is missing

1. **No 3D realization yet** — the team's stated next step, with more interaction modes (shearing, bending, twisting) untested under wave coarsening.
2. **Applications are prospective.** Multi-pattern switching, multifunctional robots, directed motion: stated aims, not demonstrations.
3. **The robustness claim is qualitative as published** — no disturbance magnitudes or failure thresholds stated; and the mechanism numbers (37 units, 24 units, ~30 Hz, 100+ sensors/actuators) reach print through consistent institutional coverage of the paper, whose full text beyond the abstract opening was not text-retrievable in this pull. It is a classical dissipative analogue, not a quantum time crystal, and the authors claim no more than that.
