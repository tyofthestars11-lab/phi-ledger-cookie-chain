# OMNI RPG: PATH OF LIGHT - the story

## Premise
The world broke into sectors and the light withdrew to the high
places. Roads forgot their destinations. But the Path of Light still
remembers itself - it is written in the one angle that never repeats
and never strays, and it rises by the golden power: sector on sector,
each 1.618 times the last.

## The Walker
A light-bearer of the old road-keepers' line. Where the Walker
gathers what the light scattered, the ground itself brightens - the
sectors respond to being walked in the true sequence.

## The cast (all voiced - Data/dialogue.csv, DT_Dialogue)
- THE KEEPER OF THE FIRST TURNING - stone keeper of Sector 1. Quest:
  five fragments, fallen where the spiral breathes out.
- THE SEER OF THE RUNGS - hears footsteps as numbers on the ladder:
  twenty, thirty-two, fifty-two. She knows the Walker's level by ear
  (the XP curve is her hearing: 20 x phi^(level-1)).
- THE CARTOGRAPHER OF THE ASCENT - mapped the Path when it was walked
  daily. His lamps are set by the golden angle; from the centroid the
  whole spiral is one gesture.
- WARDEN PRIME - guardian of the Cache of the Second Sector, where
  the ember of the old sun is sealed. Nine hundred heartbeats of
  stone. The trial is the seal.
- THE WILD MACHINES - shepherd constructs that resent the light they
  were built to serve. The OMNI faction orders, four banners.

## The acts (the quest spine in code)
1. MEET THE KEEPER - the first turning; the Path accepts the Walker.
2. GATHER FRAGMENTS - 5 fragments in Sector 1; the sector brightens.
3. MEET THE SEER - the rungs are named; the Cache's seal explained.
4. REACH THE CACHE - walk the spiral seal in the second sector.
5. THE WARDEN AWAKES - the trial of the 900 heartbeats.
6. THE PATH OPENS - the Cache opens like a sunrise remembered; the
   third sector lights its own lamps. The road climbs on.

## Form
18 voiced lines (O01-O18) ship as data: Data/dialogue.csv ->
DT_Dialogue, voices in Data/Voice (o01.wav ... o18.wav). Lines play
on quest-stage changes and on statue interaction; the Warden speaks
by trial phase. The story is the same ladder as the world: sectors,
levels, and lines all climb by phi.
