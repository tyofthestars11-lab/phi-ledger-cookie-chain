# VEIL: THE LAST SIGNAL - the story

## Premise
The district sang on every frequency until the Silence fell in a single
night. Power stayed. Machines stayed. Only the voices stopped. One
transmission still loops from the Beacon at the district's heart - the
Last Signal - and nobody left alive can hear it.

Nobody except the Runner.

## The Runner
A signal-runner: part courier, part tuning fork. The Runner hears
frequencies the way others hear weather. When the Last Signal begins
calling their name in harmonics, the district's three archivists wake.

## The cast (all voiced - Data/dialogue.csv, DT_Dialogue)
- THE KEEPER OF THE THRESHOLD - statue at the district gate. Gives the
  quest: three signal fragments, scattered when the Silence fell.
- THE SEER OF FREQUENCIES - blind archivist who sees by pitch. Her
  riddle opens the Cache: "what repeats, yet never returns the same?"
  The answer is the golden angle, walked, not spoken.
- THE CARTOGRAPHER OF THE SPIRAL - charted the district and discovered
  it was never a grid: three sectors turning at 137.508 degrees around
  the Beacon, which stands at the golden centroid.
- WARDEN PRIME - the Beacon's guardian construct, 900 heartbeats of
  stone. Not a villain: a trial. It lets nothing unmeasured pass.
- THE FOUR FACTIONS - machine orders built to guard the signal, each
  holding one fragment of the old doctrine, each having forgotten why.

## The acts
1. THE THRESHOLD - the Keeper wakes; gather 3 fragments in Sector 1
   while the factions contest the streets.
2. THE RIDDLE - the Seer; walk the spiral to open the Cache; take what
   the district was.
3. THE ALIGNMENT - the Cartographer; align the three sectors from the
   centroid; the route to the Beacon draws itself.
4. THE TRIAL - Warden Prime at the Beacon. Survive the trial measured.
5. THE CARRIER - the Beacon returns the fragments as a voice. The Last
   Signal was never lost. It was waiting for a carrier.

## Form
19 voiced lines (V01-V19) ship as data: Data/dialogue.csv ->
DT_Dialogue, voices in Data/Voice (v01.wav ... v19.wav). The quest
systems play the line for each stage; the statues speak when the
Runner interacts. Interactive: every line can be re-heard at its
statue, and the Warden's lines change with the trial's phase.
